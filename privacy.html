MultiTab Print Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

Summary

MultiTab Print does not collect, sell, or transmit personal data to its developer. Selected page content is processed locally in your browser to prepare a print document.

What the extension processes

Only after you start a print job, the extension reads the titles, addresses, visible document content, styles, and printable resources of the tabs you selected. It uses that information to assemble a local print preview. Protected browser pages, extension pages, and unsupported embedded content are skipped or reported.

Some page resources that cannot be embedded may be requested again from their original website while the preview is open. Those requests go to the website that originally supplied the resource, not to the MultiTab Print developer.

Local storage

The extension stores print preferences, pending print-job data, and a local successful-job count used to schedule optional support reminders. This information stays in your browser profile. You can clear it through your browser's extension storage controls or by uninstalling the extension.

Support links

Some browser editions show optional, dismissible donation links after occasional successful print jobs. The extension never opens a donation site automatically. If you choose a link, that site's privacy policy applies. The Safari edition contains no donation links.

Permissions

  • tabs lists tabs and identifies the tabs you select.
  • scripting reads printable content only when you request a print job.
  • storage keeps local settings, pending jobs, and reminder state.
  • contextMenus adds the tab-strip print command.
  • Optional website access is requested only for sites represented by tabs you choose to print.

No analytics or advertising

The extension contains no analytics SDK, tracking pixel, advertising network, account system, or remote executable code.

Changes and questions

Material changes will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be sent through the support contact on the extension's official store listing.
